Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change or cancel my booking?

You can change or cancel your booking through our website by logging into your account, or by calling our customer service team. Changes and cancellations are subject to our fare rules and may incur fees.

What is the baggage allowance?

Baggage allowance varies by ticket type and route. Economy passengers typically get one carry-on bag and one checked bag up to 23kg. Business class passengers enjoy increased allowances. Please check your booking confirmation for specific details.

How early should I arrive at the airport?

We recommend arriving at least 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights. This allows enough time for check-in, security screening, and boarding.

Can I select my seat in advance?

Yes, you can select your seat during booking or later through the "Manage My Booking" section on our website. Some seats may require an additional fee, especially those with extra legroom or in premium sections.
